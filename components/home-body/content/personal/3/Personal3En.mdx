## Where I'm From

I was born and raised in Kyiv.
In my first year at  NaUKMA (2020), I dropped out to focus on work — at the time, I was working as a graphic designer.

## Odesa

In 2021, during an interview, I was asked:
- Do you live in Odesa?
- No, why do you ask?
- Well, we're opening an office in Odesa.

That's how I ended up in Odesa — a German agency was opening an office there.
A month before moving, I traveled across Ukraine, staying in hostels — eight cities in a month.

Then I met Andrew, and at the beginning of 2022, I joined him at SiteForm.
Since then, I've been working on American software for construction project management.

## West

2022 — war. I spent the first months in Lviv.
Together with a friend, we organized a volunteer service — driving people from the railway station during curfew.
Later, I settled in Ivano-Frankivsk, where during the blackouts we opened a coworking space that lasted for half a year.

That's also where I found Nora — my dog.
In the summer of 2023, we moved to the Carpathians — to a rented cabin between Kosiv and Verkhovyna.

## Today

In 2024, I settled in Lviv.
