## Own Initiatives

### <CustomLink href={COWORKING_INST_URL}>Coworking</CustomLink>
*January 2023 – August 2023*

When the 2022 winter blackouts began, three like-minded friends and I opened a coworking space.
It was a bold move by inexperienced non-entrepreneurs — a lesson I value more than the money lost.
At its peak, we had 44 booked seats; one IT company rented 25 for their employees.
We closed in the summer of 2023 due to low profitability.

Reflecting on this experience, I later took courses in product design and marketing at Projector, as well as an async startup school by YC.

- Managed daily operations of the coworking
- Built and maintained the website

### Volunteering
*March 2022*

After the full-scale invasion, many people arrived at the Lviv railway station during curfew.
We created a Telegram bot to coordinate drivers and passengers.
I coordinated drivers, obtained curfew permits, helped users with edge cases, and promoted the bot among volunteers.
Volunteers met people at trains and helped them reach safe places.
